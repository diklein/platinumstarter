A photo post is `type: photo` in the frontmatter. It renders like an article without the reading time, and its excerpt is the first paragraph.

The slideshow above is a `Slideshow`: an array of images with `src` and `alt`, an optional `caption` shown under the frame, and optional `exif` shown in the lightbox the way the photos page shows it. `interval` changes the dwell time from its five-second default. Every slide opens in the lightbox, and the lightbox gallery is the whole carousel.
